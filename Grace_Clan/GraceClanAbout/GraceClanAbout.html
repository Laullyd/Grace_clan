WHO WE ARE

Who We Are

Grace Clan is a faith-driven NGO committed to touching lives through the power of God's grace. We exist to bring hope, restore dignity, and empower individuals and communities through outreach, support initiatives, and music ministry.

We believe that every life matters and that change happens when love is put into action. Through our programs and services, we strive to uplift the spiritual, emotional, and social well-being of people, especially the vulnerable and undeserved.

At Grace Clan, it's not just about what we do, but why we do it, because we are called to serve, to give, and to share the love of Christ in practical ways.

MUSIC MINISTRY

Ministering Through Songs

Grace Clan Music Ministry exists to proclaim the Gospel through heartfelt worship and
spirit-filled music. Under the leadership of our President, the ministry travels to churches,
revival meetings, crusades, and special Christian gatherings, using music as a
tool to minister God's love and grace.

We believe that worship is more than singing—it is a ministry that touches hearts, strengthens faith,
and prepares people to encounter God's presence. Every ministration is an opportunity to
encourage believers, reach the lost, and glorify Jesus Christ through songs inspired by His Spirit.
